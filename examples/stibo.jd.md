About the job

Turn AI opportunities into practical solutions with real business impact

At Stibo Systems, we are accelerating our AI journey and looking for an AI Solutions Engineer who can help transform business opportunities into scalable solutions that create measurable value.
 

You will join our Data & Digitalization team and become part of a group of specialists working across data, analytics, integrations, applications, enterprise architecture, governance and business enablement. Together, we are responsible for delivering on Stibo Systems' Data Strategy and helping the organization unlock value from data, technology and AI in close collaboration with our AI Centre of Excellence and Process Excellence. Supported by senior leadership and working closely with our AI Centre of Excellence and Process Excellence capability, you will play a key role in advancing Stibo Systems' Data and AI ambitions.

The role
In this new role as our AI Solutions Engineer, you will work closely with stakeholders across the business to identify opportunities and deliver AI-enabled capabilities that improve efficiency, enhance decision-making, and create measurable business value.

Working alongside Process Excellence, Data & Digitalization, and the AI Centre of Excellence, you will translate business challenges into practical solutions ranging from AI agents and automations to workflow improvements and enhanced access to information.

Your success will be measured by the value your solutions create and the adoption they achieve.

What you'll do
Analyze business processes and uncover opportunities to reduce manual work and improve efficiency.
Design, build, and deploy AI-powered solutions, automations, and agents.
Collaborate with architects, analysts, engineers, and integration specialists to deliver scalable solutions.
Move initiatives from experimentation to operational use and measurable impact.
Establish reusable AI patterns and best practices across the organization.
Contribute to the continued development of Stibo Systems’ AI capabilities and ways of working.
Ensure solutions align with governance, security, and responsible AI principles.
 


The Team
You will join our Data & Digitalization team based primarily in Aarhus, working alongside specialists in data, analytics, integrations, enterprise architecture, governance, and business enablement.

Together with the AI Centre of Excellence and Process Excellence, we help the business unlock value from data, technology, and AI. In this role, you will collaborate with stakeholders across our global business, helping drive AI adoption, process optimization, and operational excellence across functions and regions.

What you bring
You are a hands-on AI professional who combines technical expertise with strong business understanding. You enjoy turning complex challenges into practical solutions and working closely with stakeholders to create measurable business value.
 

Moreover, you:
Hold a Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, AI, Engineering, or a related field – or bring equivalent practical experience.
Have experience building and deploying software, automation, or AI-enabled solutions in business environments.
Have hands-on experience with APIs, system integrations, Git, and modern software development practices.
Understand technologies and concepts such as LLMs, AI Agents, RAG, Prompt Engineering, and Vector Databases.
Communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Take ownership from idea to implementation and enjoy turning business needs into scalable solutions.
