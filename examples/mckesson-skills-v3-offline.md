# McKesson Skills hierarchy - offline controlled fixture

This is a deterministic structure example for the Technical AI Enablement Senior Engineer JD. It is not a live Jev result and makes no claim about writing quality or hiring outcomes.

## Displayed Skills groups

- **Languages & interfaces:** Python, SQL, APIs
- **AI frameworks, models & providers:** LangChain, OpenAI
- **Cloud & data platforms:** Azure, AWS, Google Cloud
- **Automation & orchestration:** UiPath
- **Deployment & operations:** Docker, Kubernetes
- **Methods & practices:** MLOps, CI/CD, containerization
